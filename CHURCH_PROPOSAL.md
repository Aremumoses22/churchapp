## Executive Summary

We are presenting **Grace Connect**, a fully custom-built mobile application designed specifically for your church community. This app brings every aspect of church life into one beautiful, easy-to-use platform — right in the pocket of every member, young and old.

Whether a member wants to catch up on Sunday's sermon, register for the next event, give their tithe, or request prayer — they can do it all from their phone, anytime, anywhere.

## The Problem We're Solving

Many churches today face the same challenge: **members are scattered across multiple platforms** — one WhatsApp group for announcements, a website for events, a YouTube channel for sermons, a bank account for giving, and phone calls for prayer requests. This is:

- 😵 Confusing and overwhelming for members
- 📉 Leading to disengagement, especially among youth
- 🕐 Time-wasting for church administrators
- 📵 Completely invisible to unchurched visitors

**Grace Connect solves all of this with one app.**

## What the App Does

### 🏠 Home Dashboard
A personalized home screen that greets each member by name. It surfaces the most relevant content — today's verse, upcoming events, the latest sermon, and a quick-access toolbar for every major feature of church life.

---

### 🎙️ Sermons & Messages
- Stream full Sunday messages and mid-week teachings directly in the app
- Video and audio playback with full controls (seek, speed, skip)
- Browse sermon series with beautiful cover artwork
- Save favourite messages to listen to offline
- Share sermons with friends and family

---

### 📅 Events & Programs
- Browse all upcoming church events with images, dates, and descriptions
- View past event photo albums
- Register for events directly in the app
- Calendar view to see everything at a glance

---

### 🙏 Prayer & Community
- Submit prayer requests and have them prayed over by the church team
- Community forum for church members to encourage one another, share testimonies, and ask questions
- Post updates, like and comment — all within a safe, moderated church environment

---

### 💰 Giving & Tithes
- Give tithes, offerings, and first fruits directly through the app
- Choose specific giving categories (missions, building fund, welfare, etc.)
- View personal giving history

---

### 📖 Bible & Devotionals
- Full Bible built into the app — no internet needed
- Daily verse and devotional content
- Highlight and save favourite passages

---

### 🎵 Media & Worship
- Worship song lyrics screen for live services
- Photo gallery of church moments
- Podcast feed for teaching series

---

### 🔔 Notifications
- Instant push notifications for announcements, event reminders, and prayer updates
- Never miss an important church message

---

### 👤 Member Profile
- Personal profile with photo and membership details
- Track spiritual milestones
- Manage notification preferences and app settings

## Technical Overview

The app is built with modern, industry-grade technology:

| Component | Technology |
|---|---|
| Mobile Framework | Flutter (cross-platform: Android + iOS) |
| State Management | Riverpod |
| Navigation | Go Router |
| Video Playback | video_player + Chewie |
| Audio Playback | just_audio |
| Image Delivery | Cached Network Image |
| Authentication | Custom Auth Service |
| Local Storage | Shared Preferences |
| Backend Ready | Node.js + PostgreSQL (Prisma) |

The app is **production-ready** for Android today, and can be extended to iOS with minimal additional work. The backend is fully designed and ready to connect, enabling real member data, real sermons, real events, and real giving — all under your church's control.
